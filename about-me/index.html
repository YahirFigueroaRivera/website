# about me

## bienvenidos!

i'm a privacy advocate hardware professional with an interest in cybersecurity.

my technical skills stem from aerospace manufacturing. i have my IPC 610, WHMA, J-STD, and the space addendum.

i had my interest in technology as far as i can remember, with a tinkerer mindset as a kid, i took apart all electronics i could get my hands on (for better or for worse.) as the family tech guy, i took the opportunity to learn to fix all the electronics my family had that were broken. whether it's an old computer that's not posting, up to a portable fan with ripped pads, i learned how to fix everything with the power of youtube.

my next step in my technological journey is developing a proficiency with ki-cad, and finishing my phd in electrical engineering.
